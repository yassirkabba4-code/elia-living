Villa Ciri blends striking contemporary architecture with the natural beauty of the northern Costa Blanca, offering privileged sea views and refined Mediterranean living between Moraira and Calpe.

Newly constructed in the prestigious Racó de Galeno estate, it is designed for those seeking design, comfort and exclusivity.

01 · InteriorNoble materials, soft tones

Careful natural lighting creates an airy, open atmosphere. Floor-to-ceiling windows and large white volumes connect inside and out. The generous living room offers multiple seating areas and flows into the dining space and a contemporary kitchen, an effortless layout for daily living and entertaining.

02 · ExteriorAn infinity pool facing the Mediterranean

The main façade opens onto a spacious terrace with an infinity pool and panoramic sea views. Several garden areas and covered terraces extend the living space outdoors for family gatherings, sunset evenings and quiet moments by the pool throughout the year.

03 · LayoutComfortable circulation on every level

  • ArrivalAn elegant pedestrian entrance at the upper part of the plot, beside a covered garage for two cars.
  • ConnectionAn exterior staircase and an interior lift connect all floors.
  • Top floorThe sleeping area, with bedrooms opening onto private terraces.
  • BasementA large, flexible space for a gym, wine cellar, leisure area or extra bedroom.

04 · SurroundingsBetween Moraira and Calpe

Set in one of the most exclusive areas of the Benissa coast, with easy access to local beaches, quality restaurants and the relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle.

In summaryWhat sets it apart

  • New construction in the Racó de Galeno estate
  • Infinity pool and expansive terraces with sea views
  • Home automation and lift
  • Large adaptable basement
  • Covered garage for two vehicles
