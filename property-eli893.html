A home designed to blend contemporary luxury, nature and conscious, sustainable living, in one of the most established and prestigious areas of Balcón al Mar, where new developments are virtually non-existent.

The villa is being built on a double, completely flat plot of 2,139 m², a unique phenomenon in the hilly terrain of Jávea. It allows for a true estate: a sprawling garden with mature palms and lush vegetation that feels as if it has been growing here for a decade, a finished garden from day one.

01 · ArchitectureA contemporary home with a Mediterranean soul

The design merges the soft lines of Mediterranean architecture with a timeless contemporary style. Most functions are conveniently planned on the ground floor.

  • Living spaceA spectacular high-ceilinged lounge with vast glazing, and a kitchen with a panoramic window facing Montgó, supported by a hidden back-kitchen pantry.
  • Night zoneThree en-suite bedrooms with walk-in closets, plus a cosy guest wing with its own private terrace.
  • The Riu RauA terrace with gentle arches and a pergola is the heart of the home, capturing winter sun and giving summer shade: a modern interpretation of the traditional Spanish finca.

02 · Master suiteA private sanctuary with sea views

The entire upper floor is dedicated to the owners: a private, intimate space designed for slow mornings and afternoon silence.

  • BedroomSpacious, with panoramic windows.
  • Dressing roomA generous multi-door dressing room.
  • Bath & terracesAn elegant bathroom in natural stone, and two terraces, one facing the sea and one facing Montgó, so you can follow the light through the day.

03 · InteriorsNatural materials and a seamless flow

Luxury here is defined by authentic materials, with high-end finishes throughout.

  • StoneKitchen island and countertops in Neolith or premium travertine, fireplaces in cleaned travertine, bathrooms in stone-inspired porcelain (Pietra Edition / Marmorio).
  • WoodDark oak kitchen fronts and fluted oak entrance doors add warmth and elegance.
  • FlowContinuous flooring runs from the interior out to the terraces, so the house opens into the garden.

04 · GardenYour private boutique resort

Landscape architecture focused on privacy. Mature palms, lavender, relaxation zones with hammocks and a pool pre-installed for heating create intimate corners for outdoor living. Fully fenced and set back from the main road, the plot offers privacy rarely found in such a prime location.

05 · ComfortTechnology for year-round living

Designed as a primary residence, not only a holiday home.

  • ClimateZoned underfloor heating and ducted air conditioning discreetly hidden in the ceilings.
  • Smart homePre-installation for domotic control of lighting, blinds and air conditioning.
  • DetailsWalk-in showers with invisible drains, gunmetal fittings, and high-performance thermal and acoustic aluminium joinery.

06 · SustainabilityResponsible luxury

Sustainability is integral here, not an afterthought.

  • WaterAn ecological bio-purification system recovers 97% of water for garden irrigation.
  • EnergyHeat pump, solar water heating and pre-installation for photovoltaics.
  • MaterialsDurable natural wood and stone reduce the need for future renovation.

07 · Logistics & securityRoom to arrive

The double plot allows a comfortable logistics zone: a wide driveway, ample guest parking and a covered garage with extra storage, a solution rarely seen in Balcón al Mar. Anti-burglary doors and video intercom systems provide security.

In summaryWhat sets it apart

  • A rare, double flat plot of 2,139 m²
  • A master suite occupying the entire upper floor
  • Ecological water recovery and high energy efficiency
  • Mature vegetation with a luxury feel from day one
  • Riu Rau terraces blended with contemporary architecture
