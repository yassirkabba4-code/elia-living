Our people

Meet the founders

Karina and Monika created Elia Living with a shared passion for design, strategy and meaningful living. Their own experience as investors gives them a unique perspective: they know what it takes to make wise choices, revive homes with soul, and guide clients with clarity and care.

Karina drives Elia Living’s growth and innovation. She brings outstanding business, financial and legal expertise, driving the agency’s growth and development. Visionary and proactive, she builds strategic partnerships and creates standout opportunities. Her focus on uncompromising quality ensures the brand delivers projects that are truly exceptional.
