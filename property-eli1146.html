A sophisticated villa that blends timeless contemporary design with authentic Spanish character, set in the prestigious Balcón al Mar area of Jávea.

Fully renovated with soul, rebuilt from the ground up while preserving the original structure. Every room has its own bathroom, and every detail reflects premium craftsmanship: clean lines, neutral tones, natural textures and wood, with subtle local accents.

01 · Main levelLight, height and the view of Montgó

A bright open-plan living area with high ceilings, a fireplace and panoramic views. The fully equipped kitchen with island and built-in cabinetry connects to the dining and lounge zones and opens through a floor-to-ceiling window onto the terrace and its view of Montgó. A guest bedroom and full bathroom complete the level.

02 · Lower levelA private master suite

The master suite has a walk-in wardrobe, en-suite bathroom and its own terrace with views. Two further bedrooms, each with en-suite bathroom, ensure comfort and privacy.
