Villa Resa is a striking new-build villa in the Jazmines residential area of Cumbre del Sol, with minimalist modern architecture and uninterrupted Mediterranean sea views.

Designed across three levels, this bright, sustainable home combines refined materials with the latest energy-efficiency technologies for effortless coastal living.

01 · InteriorClean lines and natural stone

A palette of light tones and natural stone accents creates an elegant, welcoming interior. Large windows fill the open-plan living, dining and kitchen area with daylight and frame the sea.

02 · LayoutThree levels

  • Main levelOpen-plan living with central island kitchen, and the master suite with en-suite bathroom and dressing room.
  • Lower levelTwo bedrooms, each with a private bathroom and direct access to the garden.
  • Upper levelA suite with a private terrace, for guests or a quiet retreat.

03 · ExteriorAn infinity pool on the horizon

Terraces, stone walls and landscaped areas of native vegetation. The infinity pool is the visual heart of the plot and appears to merge with the horizon. An easy-to-maintain garden and several sunny terraces give varied settings for dining, relaxing and entertaining.

04 · ComfortHigh specification throughout

  • ClimateUnderfloor heating and aerothermal system.
  • EnergySolar panels and EV charging pre-installation.
  • TechnologyHome automation and high-quality carpentry throughout.

05 · SurroundingsBetween Moraira and Jávea

In an exclusive residential enclave minutes from coves, restaurants, sports facilities and an international school, with golf and coastal leisure close by.

In summaryWhat sets it apart

  • New construction with panoramic sea views
  • Ground-floor master suite with dressing room
  • Infinity pool and landscaped Mediterranean garden
  • Aerothermal system, solar panels, EV pre-installation
